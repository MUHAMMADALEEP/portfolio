About Me

My Story

I am Muhammad Noralif, a Bachelor of Electronic Industrial Automation Technology student at Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA). I have always enjoyed tinkering with electronic components, and that curiosity led me into this field.

I chose Kolej Vokasional Slim River because I found the academic subjects in secondary school hard to keep up with, and hands-on learning suited me better. My six-month internship at DLA Engineering let me explore much more, from wiring to installing access control systems on real sites.

Running my own online business taught me how to communicate with different kinds of people, a skill I use in group projects and at work.
